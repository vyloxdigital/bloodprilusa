The Silent Threat

High Blood Pressure Puts 1 in 3 American Adults at Risk

According to the CDC, nearly 48% of US adults have hypertension — yet most experience few obvious symptoms until serious complications arise. The American Heart Association reports high blood pressure contributes to over 540,000 US deaths annually.

  • Silent Symptoms

    Most people with elevated blood pressure feel completely normal. Hypertension is often called the "silent killer" because arterial damage accumulates invisibly over years.

  • Heart Strain

    Chronic high blood pressure forces the heart to work harder, thickening heart muscle walls and significantly increasing the risk of heart attack and heart failure.

  • Vascular Damage

    Sustained hypertension damages the delicate inner lining of arteries, promoting plaque buildup and stiffening that reduces blood flow to vital organs.

  • Stroke Risk

    Hypertension is the single most important modifiable risk factor for stroke. The NIH NHLBI identifies BP control as the cornerstone of stroke prevention.
